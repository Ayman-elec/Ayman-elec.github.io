01 / WIRELESS & RF SYSTEMS

Long-Range Dual-Band Robotic Radio Link & Mesh Module

In development · AI ARD Robotik
Wireless Systems Engineer — link architecture, RF hardware selection, spectrum compliance

A dual-band, dual-link communication architecture for teleoperated ground and air robots: a 2.4 GHz high-rate data plane carrying video and payload traffic, and an 868 MHz telemetry plane that stays up independently, so heartbeat and control survive loss of the high-rate link. Validated at over 1.5 km ground-to-ground.

The band plan came out of a primary-source regulatory analysis rather than a datasheet: the 5470–5725 MHz band was eliminated once national rules were found to bar its use on UAVs, and the data plane was locked to 2400–2483.5 MHz at 100 mW EIRP under ETSI EN 300 328. A link-budget comparison of roughly 52 dB between candidate sub-GHz options under EU power and duty-cycle limits then decided the telemetry radio.

The current work extends this into a multi-hop mesh module for multi-node UAV and UGV operation, with Layer-2 mesh routing on an open-MAC platform.

02 / RESEARCH · DEEP LEARNING

6G THz Channel Estimation — Deep Unfolding (LAMP)

Under review · IEEE ICEEET
Senior Project · Deep Learning Researcher — supervised by Asst. Prof. Dr. Tarik A. Almohamad

A Learned Approximate Message Passing (LAMP) network for near-field channel estimation in Terahertz ultra-massive MIMO. The work builds a three-constraint theoretical framework that predicts and explains the performance ceiling of block-diagonal analog combining — showing the limit is architectural, not algorithmic.

Channels were generated with the TeraMIMO simulator at 300 GHz over 10 GHz of bandwidth, and controlled A/B tests on identical channel realizations produced a concrete antenna-aperture design rule for sub-array receivers.

03 / AUTONOMOUS VEHICLE · POWER & EMBEDDED

Parsav — Autonomous UGV Electrical Architecture

Teknofest · Development concluded
Head of Electrical & Electronics Department
PDB — 3-output power distribution board
ELRS Tx/Rx telemetry board (ESP32)

I led the full electrical architecture for a manual and autonomous unmanned ground vehicle: power distribution, high-current supply, communication, and a fault-tolerant safety system.

The build included an 868 MHz ELRS telemetry link chosen for reliable non-line-of-sight control, a custom 3-output Power Distribution Board holding 94% measured efficiency at 95 W, and a custom 13S4P Li-Ion pack on a 200 A BMS. A matching in-house FOC BLDC motor driver (DRV8353 + STM32G431) was designed and simulated against the motors' 100 mΩ phase resistance and 48 V / 3200 RPM ratings, but not built.

04 / ROBOTICS · MECHATRONICS

Teleoperation Robot Arm — Master / Slave

Demonstration prototype · Working
Full design, CAD, and physical build — prototype built while advising a graduation project

A master–slave teleoperation system built from two semi-identical robotic arms. An operator holds and moves the master; the slave mirrors the motion in real time — reproducing the same trajectory with high precision and effectively no perceptible delay.

I designed the full mechanical assembly in CAD — custom joint housings, a rotating base, and the linkage arms — then assembled and wired the servo-driven hardware and motion-mapping control. It was built as a low-cost demonstrator, so that a student I was advising could see the master–slave mapping working end to end before committing to their own build.
